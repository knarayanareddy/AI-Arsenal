## Overview

Builds and explains neural-network training from the tensor operations upward. The teaching and the code are the same artefact: nanoGPT and the from-scratch series let you follow a complete training loop rather than infer it from a framework's internals.

## Why Follow

Useful when you want the mechanics of a transformer explained from the operations up rather than from the API surface, and when you want a training implementation small enough to read completely in an afternoon.

## Notable Work

nanoGPT, a roughly 300-line GPT implementation that trains on Shakespeare and real checkpoints; the “Let's build GPT” and “Let's build the GPT Tokenizer” series; “A Recipe for Training Neural Networks”; the tokenizer and data-preparation tooling in minbpe and llm.c.
