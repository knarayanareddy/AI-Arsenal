## Overview

AI Weekly is a free AI news-intelligence newsletter that tracks what influential AI experts and organizations are reading and sharing, then ranks and explains developments in models, agents, funding, policy, and research.

## Who it's for

Practitioners, researchers, founders, and enterprise teams who need a broad view of current AI developments without monitoring every primary source and expert account independently.

## What you'll get

- Three editions per week covering models, agents, companies, research, funding, and policy
- Ranked stories based on attention signals from influential AI people and organizations
- Links to the underlying sources for deeper reading and verification

## How to get value fast

Scan the ranked items for changes that affect your work, then open the linked primary sources for technical or strategic decisions. The public issue archive is also useful for reviewing recent developments without subscribing first.

## What to avoid

Do not treat the rankings or summaries as a replacement for the cited primary source when making high-stakes engineering, research, investment, or policy decisions.
