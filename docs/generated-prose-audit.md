## Why these exist

The 2026-06 and 2026-07 population batches were produced by generators
that filled a fixed sentence frame per section. The editorial gate at the
time rejected the frames it knew about, so these families passed: one
sentence about deployment-versus-cost comparison, one about a feature
list, one about maturity, and a limitation that asserts a hosted-only
product regardless of whether the tool is a local library. The retired
generator is in `quarantine/2026-10-24/`.

The gate now names these frames, so changed files cannot reintroduce
them. What remains is the pre-existing text, listed below and carried by
the baseline until each entry is re-authored.
